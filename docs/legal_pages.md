# MindPal Legal Pages

## Purpose

MindPal now provides public, standalone legal pages for its Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Both pages follow the product’s calm, native visual system: restrained white space, clear typography, compact navigation, and no gradients or heavy promotional cards.

## Public routes

| Route | Page | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| `/privacy` | Privacy Policy | Deployed and visually verified in production on August 16, 2026. |
| `/terms` | Terms of Service | Deployed and visually verified in production on August 16, 2026. |

## Coverage

The Privacy Policy documents Local Mode, Cloud Mode, optional memory, supported account information, user controls, security, retention, and safety-related context. The Terms cover MindPal’s service boundaries, safety notice, account and cloud-data responsibilities, acceptable use, AI-output limitations, availability, termination, and the Privacy Policy link.

Both pages include direct navigation back to MindPal, cross-links to one another, crawlable metadata, dark-mode support, keyboard focus styling, reduced-motion support, and sitemap entries.
